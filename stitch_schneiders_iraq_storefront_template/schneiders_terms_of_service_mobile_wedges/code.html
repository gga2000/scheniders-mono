1. Ordering

When you place an order, it's not locked in until we confirm it. Expect a quick phone call from our team to verify your address and details. If we can't reach you after a few tries, we might have to cancel the order.

sell

2. Pricing

All prices are in Iraqi Dinars (IQD / د.ع). We try our best to keep prices accurate, but if there's a glaring mistake (like a fridge for 1,000 IQD), we reserve the right to cancel the order and fix the typo.

payments

3. Payment

Cash on Delivery (COD) is king here. You pay when the goods arrive at your door. Please have exact change if possible, it makes everyone's life easier!

local_shipping

4. Delivery

We aim for 24-hour delivery within Baghdad, and 48-72 hours for the rest of Iraq. Sometimes traffic or weather gets in the way, but we'll keep you updated.

replay

5. Returns

Not happy? You have 7 days to initiate a return, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging. Read our full return policy here.

gavel

6. Acceptable Use

Don't be a jerk. No placing fake orders, providing bogus addresses, or harassing our staff. We will block users who abuse the system. Keep it cool.

handshake

7. Disputes

If there's a problem, let's talk it out on WhatsApp first. We almost always find a fair solution. Legally speaking, these terms are governed by the laws of Iraq.

What if something breaks? 🛠️

Many of our electronics and appliances come with a manufacturer's warranty. Check the specific product page for details. If it's dead on arrival, contact us immediately!

Accounts 👤

You don't actually need an account to shop with us! We keep it simple. If you do create one to track orders, keep your password safe. We aren't responsible if someone else uses your account.

We update these sometimes 📝

The world changes, and so do our rules. We might update these terms occasionally. If we make big changes, we'll try to let you know, but it's always good to check back here.

Still have questions? 🤔

We're real people, not robots. Drop us a message and we'll sort it out.
