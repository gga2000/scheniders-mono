# Candy Bento — playful pastels

Vibrant, joyful, mobile-first e-commerce template for Iraqi market. Mixed Arabic + English. LTR.

## Palette
- Hot pink #ff5a8a (primary CTA)
- Mint #7cf0bd (success / WhatsApp / fresh accent)
- Butter yellow #ffd84a (highlight / urgency)
- Lilac #c5b6ff (soft secondary surface)
- Sky #9ad8ff (info accent)
- Cream #fff7ed (background)
- Charcoal #1a1a1a (ink)

## Personality
Playful, candy-colored bento grids, chunky rounded shapes (12-16px radius), wobbly outlined buttons, joyful Arabic + English mixed copy. Stickers, emoji-as-icons, taped-on-paper feel. Big chunky display font (Bricolage Grotesque) for headlines, DM Sans for body. Photography is bright daylight lifestyle.

## Conversion baked in
- IQD pricing with thousands separator and 'د.ع' suffix
- COD glorified as feature, not a fallback
- WhatsApp green pill always visible
- Trust marks in pastel pill badges
- 24h delivery promise in butter yellow sticker

## Mood references
Duolingo + Liquid Death + Olipop + Notion marketing pages mixed. Big, confident, fun, never corporate.
