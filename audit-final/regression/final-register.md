## New observations

1. **Storefront orders break the courier money contract (code-proven; no live instances).** `plat/lib/order-money.ts:3-9` makes `orders.price` net of discount and has the courier collect `price + delivery_fee` (`:44`). The storefront writes `price: subtotal` (pre-discount, `sf/app/actions/checkout.ts:253,352`) and stores the discount separately in `discount_amount` (`:319,354`). Any storefront order with a coupon, bundle or points discount would be sent to the courier for `subtotal + fee`, overcharging the customer by the full discount. Live: 0 orders with `source='storefront'`; all 206 discounted orders come from other sources and 198 of them follow the net convention. The comment at `checkout.ts:324-325` cites the old convention. **Latent until storefront COD orders flow.**
2. **Any self-signup can take over all payslips.** `user_profiles.role='admin'` is written for every signup (`signup/route.ts:113`), and the `payroll-pdfs` storage policy trusts exactly that value across tenants. With NEW-50 (no email, no rate limit), one `curl` gives full ALL rights on every tenant's payslip PDFs. Bucket is empty today (0 objects), so the exposure arrives with the first payslip upload. SEC-07 is known; the signup-to-SEC-07 chain is not recorded anywhere.
3. **Migration drift is moving in both directions this week.** `activity_logs_append_only` (20261019010921) is live but not in `mig/` at the deployed commit, while two on-disk migrations (`20261012120000`, `20261014120000`) were never applied. Today's security fixes are therefore partly unreproducible from the repo, and order search runs on its fallback path.
4. **`delivery_import_items` still lands with NULL `tenant_id` after the confirm route was hardened.** 342 of 342 October items have NULL tenant while their parents are scoped. Under the `tenant_isolation_*` RLS family, those rows are invisible to the merchant who created them. This is the DATA-01 orphan count growing (859 → 1,201).
5. **Webhook "monitor mode" is fail-open by default and is the production state.** `resolveWebhookAuthMode` defaults to `"monitor"` (`plat/lib/webhook-auth.ts:173`), and none of `WEBHOOK_AUTH_MODE`, `INTERAKT_WEBHOOK_AUTH_MODE` or `LIGHTFUNNELS_WEBHOOK_AUTH_MODE` is set in Vercel platform prod. Every route adopting `enforceWebhookAuth` therefore accepts unauthenticated calls until someone flips an env var, so new code can claim to authenticate while not doing so.
